2. 服务是什么2. What the service is

Murmur 是一款私密日记应用：你在设备上记录生活与情绪，并可选择让一位或多位 AI 朋友（目前为 Wren、Sage、Pip、Rowan）对你授权可见的条目生成简短回应。这些朋友是人工智能，不是真人。它不是社交网络，不向其他真人用户展示你的内容，也不是医疗或心理服务。

Murmur is a private journal: you record life and feelings on your device, and you may invite one or more AI friends (currently Wren, Sage, Pip, and Rowan) to write a short reply to entries you make visible to them. These friends are artificial intelligence, not people. It is not a social network, it does not show your content to other human users, and it is not medical or psychological care.
