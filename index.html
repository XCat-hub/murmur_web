Murmur · 随想

只属于你的日记，
有朋友回应。

A journal that is only yours,
with friends who reply.

写下生活与情绪。Luna、Atlas、Milo 会用不同的方式接住你。没有其他真人，没有关注，没有广场——像一本只给你看的朋友圈。

Write what happened, and how it felt. Luna, Atlas, and Milo meet you in different ways. No other people, no followers, no public square — like a feed that exists only for you.

隐私优先

Private first

日记默认只存在你的设备上，本地数据库加密。没有广告，不出售数据，不用你的内容训练通用模型。

Your journal stays on your device by default, in an encrypted local database. No ads, no selling data, and we do not use your writing to train a general model.

你决定谁看见

You choose who sees it

每条记录都可以选择哪些 AI 朋友可见。没被选中的朋友既不会回应，也不会把这条写进记忆。

Each entry can be visible only to the AI friends you choose. The others will not reply, and will not remember that entry.

不是社交产品

Not a social network

没有其他用户，没有公开分享。随想解决的是「想被理解，但不想被围观」。

There are no other users and no public sharing. Murmur is for being understood without being watched.

不是心理治疗

Not therapy

AI 朋友提供陪伴和视角，不做诊断，不替代专业帮助。若你正处于危机中，请向真人与专业热线求助。

AI friends offer company and perspective. They do not diagnose, and they are not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis, please reach a person you trust or a local helpline.

三位朋友

Three friends

回应很短，像朋友的一句评论，而不是长篇分析。你也可以只写日记、不请任何人看。

Replies are short, like a friend’s comment — not a long analysis. You can also keep a private diary and invite no one.

Luna

安慰者

The Comforter

温暖、善良，永远先接住你的情绪。不说教，不急着给建议。

Warm and kind. She meets the feeling first — no lectures, no rush to advice.

Atlas

观察者

The Observer

看见你反复提起的事，帮你看到更大的图景。不施压，不揣测动机。

He notices what returns, and the larger picture. No pressure, no guessing at motives.

Milo

乐观者

The Optimist

在合适的时候点出一点亮处。你低落时，他不会硬撑「想开点」。

He finds a light touch when it fits. When you are low, he will not tell you to cheer up.

关于 AI 回应。 日记本身保存在本机。只有当你请朋友回应时，相关文字才会经我们的服务器临时发送给 AI 服务以生成短评。代理不保存原文。图片留在设备上，不会上传。登录 Google 或 Apple 只是为了给 AI 代理一个稳定身份，日记不会随这个账号同步。

About AI replies. The journal itself stays on your device. Only when you invite a friend to reply is the relevant text sent, through our server, to an AI service to generate a short comment. The proxy does not keep the original text. Photos stay on the device and are not uploaded. Signing in with Google or Apple only gives the AI proxy a stable identity; your journal does not sync with that account.
